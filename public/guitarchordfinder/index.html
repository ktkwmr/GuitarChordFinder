Guitar Chord Finder

Find how to play chords in alternate guitar tunings such as DADGAD, Open G, and Drop D, in low, middle, and high positions. Tap a diagram to hear it. You can also name a chord from the frets you press. Free, no sign-up, and it works offline.

Tap the fretboard to choose a fret on each string. The buttons on the left switch a string between open (○) and muted (×). The black dot is the lowest note. Tap the same fret again to go back to open.

Everything runs in your browser. No account or data upload.

How to use

  1. Tuning – Choose a preset such as DADGAD or Open G, or set each string yourself. Below the strings you can see the chord the open strings make.
  2. Chord – Choose the root and the chord type. For a slash chord such as D/F#, also choose the bass note.
  3. Shapes – Up to six natural shapes are found for each position: low (open strings), middle (around frets 3–6), and high (fret 7 and up). Tap “Other shape” to see the next one.
  4. Listen – Tap a diagram to hear it. Under Playback, choose note by note, chord, or both.
  5. Shape → chord – Press frets on the fretboard to see the name of the chord, with other possible names.

Privacy

  • Everything stays on your device – Chords and shapes are calculated and played only in your browser. Nothing you choose is sent to a server. This app has no accounts, cookies, or ads.
  • Data saved in your browser – To work offline, the app is saved in your browser's storage. Your tuning, chord, playback mode, and the shape on the fretboard are also saved there, so you can pick up where you left off next time. You can delete them by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.
  • Server logs and page views – This site is hosted on Cloudflare. Standard access logs (such as IP address, time, and the requested page) are kept for operation and security. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views without cookies (the page URL, referrer, browser information, and page load time).

  • Full policy – See the privacy policy and about this site.
